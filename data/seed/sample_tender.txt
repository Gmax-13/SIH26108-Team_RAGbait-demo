TENDER DOCUMENT — SUPPLY, INSTALLATION, TESTING AND COMMISSIONING OF
INTERNAL ELECTRIFICATION WORKS FOR ADMINISTRATIVE BLOCK

Tender Reference: EE/ELECT/2026-27/114
Issued by: Office of the Executive Engineer (Electrical Division)

SECTION III — TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

3.1  GENERAL
3.1.1  All electrical wiring installation work shall be carried out in
       accordance with the Code of Practice for Electrical Wiring
       Installations, IS 732, and the relevant statutory rules in force.
3.1.2  The entire installation shall be earthed in accordance with the Code
       of Practice for Earthing, IS 3043 - 1987.

3.2  CABLES AND CONDUCTORS
3.2.1  All internal wiring shall be carried out using PVC insulated copper
       conductor cables of 1100 V grade conforming to IS 694.
3.2.2  Heavy duty PVC insulated armoured cables for sub-mains distribution
       shall conform to IS 1554 (Part 1) for working voltages up to and
       including 1100 V.
3.2.3  Cross-linked polyethylene insulated cables, where specified for
       outdoor feeder runs, shall conform to IS 7098 (Part 1).
3.2.4  The minimum cross sectional area of any conductor used for light
       points shall not be less than 1.5 sq mm copper.

3.3  CONDUITS AND ACCESSORIES
3.3.1  Rigid non-metallic conduits used for surface, recessed and concealed
       conduit wiring shall conform to IS 9537 (Part 3).
3.3.2  Fittings and accessories for rigid steel conduits shall conform to
       IS 3419.

3.4  SWITCHES, SOCKETS AND PROTECTION
3.4.1  All switches for domestic and similar purposes shall conform to
       IS 3854.
3.4.2  Plugs and socket outlets of rated voltage up to and including 250 V
       shall conform to IS 1293.
3.4.3  Low voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies (distribution
       boards) shall be factory built assemblies conforming to
       IS 8623 (Part 1).
3.4.4  Miniature circuit breakers shall provide overcurrent protection
       appropriate to the connected load and the conductor size.

3.5  LIGHTING
3.5.1  All luminaires shall be LED type. Self-ballasted LED lamps for general
       lighting services shall conform to IS 16102 (Part 1).
3.5.2  LED luminaires shall carry valid BIS registration under the applicable
       compulsory registration requirements.
3.5.3  Illumination levels for office areas shall comply with the national
       lighting code requirements for interior illumination.

3.6  TESTING AND COMMISSIONING
3.6.1  Insulation resistance of the completed installation shall be measured
       and recorded before energisation.
3.6.2  Earth resistance of each earth electrode shall be measured and shall
       not exceed the value stipulated in the relevant Indian Standard.
3.6.3  All high voltage switchboards shall be tested for dielectric strength
       in the manner recommended in the applicable Indian Standard.

SECTION IV — COMMERCIAL TERMS

4.1  Earnest Money Deposit of Rs 2,00,000 shall accompany the bid.
4.2  Payment shall be released within 45 days of certified completion.
4.3  The defect liability period shall be 12 months from the date of handing
     over. Any dispute shall be referred to arbitration under the
     Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
4.4  Bidders must have completed at least two similar works of value not less
     than Rs 50,00,000 in the last five years.
